Bloom representations are consistent across modalities. A UMAP is fit on Bloom representations of RNA-only inputs (genes only) and then applied to representations of ATAC-only inputs (peaks only) for the same cells. Axes are the two UMAP dimensions. Each cell is matched across modalities by its ID; positions between the two modalities are interpolated for display.
