Bloom learns consistent representations across context sizes. A joint UMAP is fit on cell representations obtained with different input contexts. The input context for each cell consists of its n most expressed genes, with n ∈ {500, 1,000, 2,000, 4,000, 8,000}. The representation is remarkably robust over this 16× range of context sizes.Axes are the two UMAP dimensions. Each cell is matched across contexts by its ID; positions between measured contexts are interpolated for display.
